What's moving above you?

Finds the patch of sky straight above you right now and shows it as SPHEREx recorded it on earlier dates. It is not a live view. Your location stays on this device; only the sky patch, rounded to 0.2 degrees, is used.

      About

      Clyde Tombaugh found Pluto in 1930 by flicking between two photographs of the same stars and spotting the one that moved. Tombaugh does the same with images from NASA's SPHEREx space telescope, for the patch of sky above you.
